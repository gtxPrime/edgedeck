Edge Deck is a Samsung-style edge panel for any Android phone. Open a sidebar of apps, tools and contacts from a screen edge or around your notch, and search everything from one box.

Get it on Google Play Free to use. Rated 4.7 on Google Play.

What it does

One panel for the things you reach for all day.

Universal search

Search apps, contacts, Google, YouTube, the Play Store, or use voice. Type a number to Call, SMS or WhatsApp in one tap.

Inline calculator

Type a math expression in the search bar and get the answer right there.

Edge or notch

Use a left or right edge handle, or place the trigger around your notch or punch-hole camera. Size, radius and opacity are adjustable.

14 utility tiles

Quick tools in the panel, such as a QR scanner, timer and more. Show, hide and reorder what you use.

Shortcuts

Pin apps, tools and contacts so they are one swipe away.

Gestures

Tap, double-tap and swipe actions on the handle to open the panel or run a tool.

Encrypted clipboard history

Clipboard history is stored on your device and encrypted with AES-256.

Black and white

A pure black look that suits AMOLED screens and stays out of the way.

FAQ

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone, not only Samsung. Install it from Google Play and follow the setup screen.

Why does it ask for permissions?

The panel needs the accessibility service to open over other apps. Other permissions, like contacts and phone, are only needed for the features that use them.
